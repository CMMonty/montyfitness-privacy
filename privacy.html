Privacy Policy

MontyFitness · Effective July 1, 2026 · Provider: Monty McCollum, operating "MontyFitness" · Contact: monty.mccollum@gmail.com

1. Who this applies to

This policy explains what data the App collects, how we use it, who we share it with, and your choices. It applies to your use of the MontyFitness mobile app and its backend service. By using the App you agree to this policy.

2. Information we collect

You give us:

  • Account info: name, email address, and a password (stored only as a secure hash — we never store your actual password).
  • Profile / goals: optional location and ZIP, timezone, age, gender, height, current and goal weight, activity level, and training frequency — used to calculate calorie/energy targets.
  • Health & fitness data you log: workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations), body weight and body-composition measurements, and nutrition/diary entries (foods, calories, macros).
  • Content you create: workout programs, plans, custom exercises, custom foods, saved meals, and — for trainers — plans you choose to submit for community sharing.

From Apple Health (only if you connect it): with your permission, we read body weight, active and resting energy, sleep, body-fat percentage, and workout data from Apple Health to display and inform your metrics. See Section 5.

Automatically: device & diagnostic data — app/OS version, device type, IP address, and error/crash diagnostics, used to keep the App reliable. We scrub authentication tokens, request contents, and health values from these diagnostics.

We do NOT collect: payment/financial data, precise/GPS or background location, advertising identifiers, or any cross-app tracking data. We do not use advertising or ad-tracking SDKs.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the App: authenticate you, save and show your workouts/nutrition/metrics, and compute targets and progress.
  • To send transactional email (email verification and password reset).
  • To keep the App secure and reliable (diagnostics, abuse/rate-limit protection).

We use your information only to operate and improve the App. We do not sell your data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use it for cross-app tracking.

4. How we share your information

We share data only with service providers that help us run the App ("sub-processors"), under agreements that limit their use of it:

  • Email delivery (Resend): receives your name and email address to send verification/reset emails.
  • Error monitoring (Sentry): receives diagnostic/crash data (with tokens, request bodies, and health values scrubbed).
  • Nutrition databases (USDA FoodData Central, FatSecret, Open Food Facts): receive the food-search terms and barcodes you enter, to return nutrition information. Food data is "Powered by FatSecret."
  • Hosting: your data is stored on our own hosting infrastructure in the United States. We will update this section if we move to a managed hosting provider.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or in connection with a business transfer. We do not sell personal or health data, and we do not share it with data brokers.

5. Apple Health (HealthKit)

If you connect Apple Health, we read the categories listed above only to provide health and fitness features inside the App. Consistent with Apple's requirements, we do not use Apple Health data for advertising or marketing, we do not sell it or share it with data brokers, and we do not disclose it to third parties without your consent. You can disconnect Apple Health at any time in your device settings; data already imported can be removed via the deletion options in Section 8.

6. Consumer Health Data (Washington "My Health My Data" Act and similar laws)

Much of what the App stores — your workouts, nutrition, body measurements, and Apple Health metrics — is consumer health data. We describe how we handle it in our separate Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy. In short: we collect it to provide the App's core features, at your direction; we do not sell it; and we share it only with the operational providers in Section 4. Where the law requires, we obtain your consent before collecting or sharing consumer health data, and you may withdraw consent and request deletion (Section 8).

7. Data retention

We keep your data while your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete your personal and health data from the live system (backups age out on our normal backup cycle). Hashed security tokens expire automatically.

8. Your rights and choices

  • Access / export: export your workouts, nutrition, body metrics, and health series as CSV from Profile → Privacy → Export.
  • Deletion: request deletion of your account and associated data. Self-serve in-app account deletion is being added; until then, contact us at the email above.
  • Correction: edit your profile, goals, and logged data in the App.
  • Consent withdrawal: disconnect Apple Health and stop providing data at any time.

Depending on where you live (e.g., California/CCPA, Washington/MHMDA, and similar), you may have additional rights to access, delete, correct, or restrict processing, and to not be discriminated against for exercising them. To make a request, contact monty.mccollum@gmail.com.

9. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit, hashed passwords and tokens, access controls, and regular backups. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will notify you and regulators of a breach as required by law.

10. Children

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect data from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. California privacy rights (CCPA/CPRA)

California residents may request access to, deletion of, and correction of their personal information, and may limit certain uses of sensitive personal information. We do not "sell" or "share" personal information as those terms are defined under California law. To exercise these rights, contact monty.mccollum@gmail.com.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we will post the new version with a revised effective date and, for material changes, provide additional notice in the App or by email.

13. Contact

Questions or requests: monty.mccollum@gmail.com — Monty McCollum (MontyFitness).
