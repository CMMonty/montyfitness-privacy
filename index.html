Privacy at a glance

MontyFitness · Effective July 1, 2026

Your data, in short. The full details are in the two policies linked below.

What we collect

Your account info (name, email), the workouts, nutrition, and body data you log, and — only if you connect it — health metrics from Apple Health (weight, energy, sleep, body composition).

Why

To run the App for you: save and show your training, nutrition, and progress, and calculate your targets. That's it.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data, we don't use it for advertising, and we don't track you across other apps. Apple Health data is used only for your health and fitness inside the App.

Who touches it

Only the services we need to run the App — secure hosting, crash monitoring with your health values removed, and email for sign-in — never data brokers or advertisers.

Your controls

Export your data anytime (Profile → Privacy → Export), delete your account, correct what you've logged, and disconnect Apple Health whenever you want.
