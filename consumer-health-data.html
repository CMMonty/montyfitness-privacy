Consumer Health Data Privacy Policy

LostNotLazy · Effective November 1, 2026 · Provider: Monty McCollum, operating "LostNotLazy" · Contact: support@lostnotlazy.com

This standalone policy is required by Washington's My Health My Data Act (MHMDA), Nevada's SB 370, and provided for residents of other states with consumer-health-data rights. It covers only consumer health data. For everything else, see the Privacy Policy.

1. What we mean by "consumer health data"

Data that identifies your past, present, or future physical or mental health status. In LostNotLazy this includes your body measurements (weight, body-fat %, lean mass), workout activity (exercises, sets, reps, weights, durations), nutrition/diet (foods, calories, macros), Apple Health metrics you choose to sync (weight, active/resting energy, sleep, body composition, workouts), and health-related profile inputs (age, sex, height, goal weight, activity level) used to compute your targets.

2. Where it comes from (sources)

  • You — what you enter and log in the App.
  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — only if you connect it, and only the categories above, read on your device with your permission.

3. Why we collect and use it (purposes)

To provide the App's core features: save and display your training, nutrition, and body data; calculate calorie/energy targets and progress; and show trends. We use your consumer health data only to operate and improve these features at your direction. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not profile you for any purpose unrelated to the App.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell consumer health data, and we do not share it to any third party for their own purposes. We share it only with the service providers necessary to run the App, and only as needed:

Amazon Web Services (Lightsail, United States East region)All of your data, including consumer health data, stored at rest on an encrypted disk; nightly backups are encrypted before storageTo store and serve your account under our own account
Sentry (error monitoring)None — health values are scrubbed from diagnostics before they are sentReliability / crash diagnostics

We do not share consumer health data with data brokers, advertisers, or for any cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. Consent

  • We collect consumer health data with your consent, given by your affirmative choices in the App (creating an account, logging data, and — for Apple Health — granting HealthKit permission).
  • We would obtain separate consent before sharing your consumer health data for any purpose beyond the operational providers listed in Section 4, and a separate written authorization before any sale — but we do not sell consumer health data, so no such authorization is requested.
  • You may withdraw consent at any time (Section 6).

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • Confirm whether we collect, share, or sell your consumer health data (we collect it; we don't sell it).
  • Access your consumer health data — export it as CSV in Profile → Privacy → Export.
  • Delete your consumer health data — delete your account and all associated data yourself in the App under Privacy. You can also email support@lostnotlazy.com.
  • Withdraw consent — disconnect Apple Health in your device settings and stop logging; email us to revoke further processing.
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

To exercise any right, contact support@lostnotlazy.com. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. You may authorize an agent to act on your behalf. If we deny a request, you may appeal by replying to our response, and you may contact your state Attorney General.

7. Security & retention

We protect consumer health data with encryption in transit, access controls, and backups, and retain it while your account is active; on deletion we remove it from the live system (backups age out on our normal cycle). See the Privacy Policy for more.

8. Changes

We will post updates here with a revised effective date and provide additional notice for material changes.
